# ACEIT privacy policy (draft)

*Draft for legal review. Effective date: [date]. Operator: [legal name of the person or company that runs ACEIT], [address], [contact email].*

## 1. Who is responsible for your information

When ACEIT is used by a school, the **school decides** why and how its students' information is used (it is the "controller" or "responsible organisation"), and ACEIT handles it on the school's behalf (see the data processing agreement). ACEIT is directly responsible only for the accounts of people who use ACEIT without a school, and for running the service securely.

## 2. What we collect

- Your email address and the display name you choose.
- Which classes you joined, and the school each class belongs to.
- Your study activity: topics read, flashcards reviewed, quiz answers, games played, and recent practice-exam scores.
- A summary of that activity: percent complete, mastery, and the last time you opened ACEIT.
- A profile photo, if you choose to add one.
- Messages you choose to send as feedback.
- Technical error reports: an error message and the page it happened on.
- Content written by instructors for their class (notes, flashcards, questions, announcements, lectures and pictures). This is the instructors' own work and is shown to the members of that class.
- A record of actions taken by instructors, school managers and administrators (who did what, and when).

We do not collect your date of birth, home address, phone number, location, or grades from your school.

## 3. Why we use it

To let you log in, show the right course, remember where you stopped, schedule revision, show your instructor how the class is doing, keep the service secure, and improve it. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling. There is no analytics or advertising code on the site.

## 4. Who can see it

- You can see all of it.
- Instructors of a class you joined, and the managers of that class's school, can see your name, email, profile photo, progress, weak topics, flashcards marked as still being learned, recent practice-exam scores, and summary for that class.
- Platform administrators can see the same class information across schools to administer ACEIT.
- People in other schools, and other students, cannot.
- The people who operate ACEIT can see account information when needed to run and protect the service.
- The services in `SUBPROCESSORS.md` store and process it on our behalf.

## 5. Where it is stored

In a cloud database operated by [provider] in the region [region]. It is encrypted in transit and at rest.

## 6. How long we keep it

While your account exists. When you ask to delete your account, it is permanently deleted after 22 days (logging in again with the same email in that time cancels the deletion); your profile, progress and class memberships are then deleted. A school can ask us to remove a class or its students. Inactive accounts may be deleted after [period]. Backups are kept for [period] and then expire. (See `RETENTION-AND-DELETION.md`.)
