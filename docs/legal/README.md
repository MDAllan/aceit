# Legal drafts

> **DRAFTS for review by a lawyer. This is not legal advice.** They describe what ACEIT actually does today (see `docs/SECURITY.md`) so that a lawyer has accurate facts to start from. Square brackets mark things only you can fill in.

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `PRIVACY-POLICY.md` | What the public privacy notice should say (the app has a shorter in-app version at `#/privacy`) |
| `TERMS-OF-USE.md` | Terms for schools, instructors and students |
| `DATA-PROCESSING-AGREEMENT.md` | The school is the controller of student information; ACEIT processes it for the school |
| `SUBPROCESSORS.md` | Services ACEIT uses to run, and what they see |
| `RETENTION-AND-DELETION.md` | How long data is kept and how it is removed |
| `INCIDENT-RESPONSE.md` | What happens if something goes wrong |

Questions to put to a lawyer or to the school's privacy office:

1. Which privacy law applies to each school (public-sector law for universities, colleges and school boards; private-sector law otherwise), and does the school need a **privacy impact assessment** before a pilot?
2. Is it acceptable for student information to be stored with a cloud provider, and in which region? (Region is recorded in `SUBPROCESSORS.md`.)
3. Are any users under 18 or under the age of consent, so that parental or guardian consent is needed?
4. Who owns the course material and figures (the instructor, the school, a publisher), and what permission is needed to show them?
5. Whether the school will sign the agreement, or has its own template that replaces `DATA-PROCESSING-AGREEMENT.md`.
