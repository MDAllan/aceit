# Data processing agreement (draft)

*Draft for legal review. Between [School] (the "School") and [ACEIT operator] ("ACEIT"). Replace this with the School's own template if it has one.*

1. **Roles.** The School decides why and how its students' and staff members' personal information is used. ACEIT handles that information only on the School's behalf and instructions.
2. **What is handled.** Names, email addresses, class memberships, study activity and summaries, feedback messages, and a record of staff actions, for the School's users. The categories are listed in `docs/SECURITY.md`, section 2. No sensitive categories are collected.
3. **Purpose.** Providing ACEIT to the School's classes: sign-in, showing course content, saving progress, and dashboards for the School's instructors and managers.
4. **School's responsibilities.** To have the authority to share the information with ACEIT; to tell its users how it is used; to name its school managers; to remove staff access when someone leaves; to obtain any consent the law requires.
5. **ACEIT's responsibilities.**
   - Use the information only for the purpose above, never for advertising or sale.
   - Keep it confidential, and limit access to people who need it.
   - Apply the security measures in `docs/SECURITY.md`, and not reduce them without telling the School.
   - Use only the sub-processors in `SUBPROCESSORS.md` and give the School [30] days' notice of any change, with a right to object.
   - Help the School answer requests from individuals to access, correct or delete their information, within [10] business days.
   - Tell the School without undue delay, and within [72] hours, after becoming aware of a breach affecting its information, with the facts known (see `INCIDENT-RESPONSE.md`).
6. **Location.** The information is stored in [region]. ACEIT will not move it to another country without the School's written agreement. [Adjust to the applicable law.]
7. **Return and deletion.** On request, or within [30] days after the agreement ends, ACEIT will export the School's data and delete it, including from backups as they expire ([period]), and confirm in writing.
8. **Audit.** ACEIT will answer reasonable security questionnaires and, once a year at the School's request, provide evidence of the controls in `docs/SECURITY.md` (including the automated test results).
9. **Term.** For as long as the School uses ACEIT, plus the deletion period.
10. **Contacts.** School privacy contact: [name, email]. ACEIT contact: [name, email].
