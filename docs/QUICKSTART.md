# ACEIT quick start

One page for each person. Share the part that applies.

## For a student (1 minute)

1. Open ACEIT. Tap **New here? Sign up** (or log in if you already have an account). Use your school email if you were told to.
2. Tap **Join a class** and enter the code from your instructor.
3. Start with **Notes**, then **Cards**, then a **Quiz**. Games are under each lecture.
4. Your profile (top right) lets you change your name and look, download your data, leave a class or delete your account.

Your instructor can see your name, email and progress in their class. Other students cannot.

## For an instructor (5 minutes)

1. Your school manager adds your email address. Sign up (or log in) with that address and confirm it if asked. A **Teaching** tab appears.
2. **Teaching** lists your classes. Open one:
   - **Join code**: copy it, or copy a ready-made message for students. Change it any time (6+ characters, or press Random).
   - **Exam date**: students then see a countdown and a study plan.
   - **Students**: tap a name to see progress per lecture. Search and sort. Download a CSV.
   - **Class insights**: the questions students find hardest, and who has been quiet for a week.
3. If a student should not be in the class, open their row and press **Remove from class**.

You can also read the course as a student would: use the **Classes** tab.

### Add your own content (10 minutes)

Open a class, then **Class content**. You can add:
- **Questions, flashcards and notes** to any of the course's lectures. They appear at the end of that lecture's quiz, cards and notes, for your class only.
- **Your own lectures**, each with notes, flashcards and questions, which appear in your class's lecture list marked as added by you.
- **Announcements**: a message at the top of the class page, with an optional last day.

How it works:
1. Everything starts as a **draft**. In the Classes tab your drafts show with a Draft label, so you can try them as a student would. Students never see drafts.
2. Press **Publish** when an item is ready. If your school asks a manager to approve first, the button says **Submit for approval** and the item goes live once it is approved.
3. **Paste many** lets you copy questions from a Word document (a star after the right option, or a line like `Answer: b`) or flashcards from a spreadsheet (two columns).
4. Add a picture to a question or a note: choose a file, then describe it for students who cannot see it. Pictures are resized for you and stored privately for your class.
5. Starting a new term? **Copy for a new term** on the class page, or **Copy from another class** under Class content, brings your lectures, notes, flashcards and questions along as drafts.

Your words are shown to students exactly as you write them. Put two stars on each side of a word to highlight it. Only add pictures you have the right to use.

## For a school manager (10 minutes)

1. The ACEIT team adds your email as the manager of your school. Sign up (or log in) with that address. A **School** tab appears.
2. **Classes > New class**: choose the course, name it (for example "ERTH 209 Section 2") and optionally set a student limit. A random join code is made for you.
3. **Instructors > Invite an instructor**: enter their email and pick the class. If they have no account yet, copy the message ACEIT shows and send it.
4. Watch **Classes** for progress and activity across the school; **Download all students (CSV)** for a spreadsheet; **Activity** for the record of who changed what.
5. **Approvals** is where you decide whether instructors' own questions, flashcards, notes and lectures need your approval before students see them, and where waiting items are approved or sent back. What you publish yourself goes live directly.
6. At the end of a term, open **Class settings** and tick **Archive** to close the class to new students. **Copy for a new term** (in the same panel) makes the next term's class with the same instructors and content.
