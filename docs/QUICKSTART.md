# ACEIT quick start

One page for each person. Share the part that applies.

## For a student (1 minute)

1. Open ACEIT. Tap **New here? Sign up** (or log in if you already have an account). Use your school email if you were told to.
2. Tap **Join a class** and enter the code from your instructor.
3. Start with **Notes**, then **Cards**, then a **Quiz**. Games are under each lecture.
4. Your profile (top right) lets you change your name and look, download your data, leave a class or delete your account.

Your instructor can see your name, email and progress in their class. Other students cannot.

## For an instructor (5 minutes)

1. Your school manager adds your email address. Sign up (or log in) with that address and confirm it if asked. A **Teaching** tab appears.
2. **Teaching** lists your classes. Open one:
   - **Join code**: copy it, or copy a ready-made message for students. Change it any time (6+ characters, or press Random).
   - **Exam date**: students then see a countdown and a study plan.
   - **Students**: tap a name to see progress per lecture. Search and sort. Download a CSV.
   - **Class insights**: the questions students find hardest, and who has been quiet for a week.
3. If a student should not be in the class, open their row and press **Remove from class**.

You can also read the course as a student would: use the **Classes** tab.

## For a school manager (10 minutes)

1. The ACEIT team adds your email as the manager of your school. Sign up (or log in) with that address. A **School** tab appears.
2. **Classes > New class**: choose the course, name it (for example "ERTH 209 Section 2") and optionally set a student limit. A random join code is made for you.
3. **Instructors > Invite an instructor**: enter their email and pick the class. If they have no account yet, copy the message ACEIT shows and send it.
4. Watch **Classes** for progress and activity across the school; **Download all students (CSV)** for a spreadsheet; **Activity** for the record of who changed what.
5. At the end of a term, open **Class settings** and tick **Archive** to close the class to new students.
